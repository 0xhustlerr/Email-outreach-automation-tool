# A contact is emailed at most once

Once any Sender has emailed a contact (any send_log entry for the address), no new Opener is ever queued or sent to that contact again. The rule is enforced three times — enqueueSequence refuses at queue-add, a sweep at every worker arm cancels legacy pending rows in bulk, and the worker re-checks each Opener right after its claim — and there is deliberately no bypass: the old allowResend escape hatch, which the re-engage-from-history flow used to target past contacts from a different Sender, is gone, along with the "re-send must use a DIFFERENT Sender" rule that existed to serve it (lastSenderForContact, hasDifferentFreeSender, the waitingForSender status). We chose the hard rule over per-flow exceptions because every exception reopens the double-email hazard the send_log guard exists to prevent, and a contact who never replied has already answered. Consequences: the Pitch and the Bump still send — the rule governs starting contact, not continuing a Sequence's thread; dropped rows are closed out in cancelSequence's terminal shape (failed, reason "already emailed") so a retry can still deliberately un-cancel one; and Sender rotation, no longer needing to avoid a contact's previous Sender, simply picks the eligible Sender idle longest (least-recently-used), maximizing every Sender's own gap between sends.
